# Brand usage examples

This document demonstrates correct and incorrect use of Base brand colours and logos.

## Colours

- **Correct:** Use primary blue (#0052FF) for buttons and links.
- **Incorrect:** Changing the primary blue to a different hue.

## Logo placement

- **Correct:** Logo centered on a white or light background with minimum clear space.
- **Incorrect:** Distorting, rotating or adding effects to the logo.

See `/assets` for SVG and PNG examples.
